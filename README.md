# Objective:
To analyze road accident data to uncover trends, identify high-risk factors (vehicle type, road conditions, lighting, areas), and provide actionable insights for reducing casualties and improving road safety.

# Project Description:
This dashboard presents a comprehensive visual analysis of road accident data using Excel charts and KPIs. It highlights total accidents, severity classification, monthly trends, road and light conditions, and area-wise distribution. The goal is to assist policymakers, traffic departments, and road safety officials in making informed decisions.

# Key Insights
Cars caused the highest number of casualties: 27,539.

Slight injuries dominate the dataset, making up 87.85% of total cases.

Fatal accidents are very low, only 0.65% (244 cases).

Serious injuries account for 11.5% of casualties.

Urban areas had nearly all reported casualties: 37,590.

Single carriageways are the most dangerous road type, with 30,080 casualties.

Daylight conditions saw the majority of accidents: 26,960 cases.

Darkness contributed to 10,630 casualties.

Most accidents happened on dry roads (28,173), not during rain or snow.

Accidents occurred steadily across months, peaking between April to October.

Bicycle accidents are relatively high: 3,955 casualties.

Goods vehicles and buses show lower but still notable casualty numbers.

December shows a slight drop in accident numbers compared to other months.
